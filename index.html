Release Desk

Six small tools that share one release. Set the basics here, then work left to right; whatever you do in one tool is waiting in the next. Nothing is generated by AI: every result is rule-based, seeded and repeatable.

This release
Carry it around

release.json is the whole record: fields, palette, tracks, prompts, shortlist, series and the cover image. Open it on another machine and everything is where you left it.

“Next release” keeps the artist, palette, type and series, bumps the catalogue number and clears the title, tracks and cover, so the new sleeve starts out matching the last.

1 · Name it

Title Lab

Rule-based names for releases and tracks, from theme word banks and templates.

 2 · Colour it

Palette Pull

Ground, ink and spot from your cover, checked for contrast.

 3 · Front sleeve

Sleeve Press

One template, every release. Motif, type, image and catalogue plate.

 4 · Back sleeve

Tracklist Typesetter

Paste a list, get a back cover in the same type system.

 5 · Sound it

Style Forge

Suno style prompts from toggles, with locks, seeds and per-track tempo and key.

 6 · Ship it

Release Kit

Every size, the text files and release.json, zipped by catalogue number.
